# Welcome to the DREAM data stack

[DREAM](https://wearedream.org/) is a network of charter schools and youth programs in East Harlem and the South Bronx: schools where every child is known, seen, and loved, wrapped in extended-day sports, enrichment, and family programming. **Play. Learn. Grow.**

This is where our data & AI team builds the machinery behind all of it.

## What lives here

**AI agents & bots.** Coaches and deans talk to an AI observation agent in chat; it turns classroom walkthrough notes into polished, rubric-aligned feedback for teachers in minutes, and logs every observation for coaching analytics. More agents are always in the lab.

**Data pipelines & infrastructure.** A cloud data warehouse stitching together our student information system, behavior and culture platforms, assessments, enrichment rosters, HR, and more: hundreds of curated, version-controlled models refreshing around the clock.

**Dashboards & analytics.** A self-hosted data hub that puts live answers in front of principals, coaches, and program leaders: arc-of-the-year goals, observation coverage, afterschool enrollment and attendance, operations KPIs, and beyond.

**Automated messaging.** Daily attendance digests, behavior-referral alerts, and program health checks that land in the right inboxes at the right time, so humans spend their energy on kids instead of spreadsheets.

**And yes**, our repositories are named after DREAM's maxims: *All Kids Can. Teamwork Makes the DREAM Work. Fun Is a Serious Value. Fail, Persist, Exceed. DREAM Is Family.* We take the mission seriously; ourselves, less so.

## New to the team?

Welcome home! Getting set up:

1. [Download PyCharm Community Edition](https://www.jetbrains.com/pycharm/download/) (or your editor of choice).
2. On PyCharm's welcome screen choose **Get from VCS** (or *File > Project from Version Control*), pick **Git**, and paste the repo URL, e.g. `https://github.com/DREAM-Charter-Schools/All-Kids-Can.git`.
   * *Repo not found?* You need a personal access token: GitHub portrait (top right) > **Settings > Developer settings > Tokens (classic) > Generate new token**. Name it, set an expiration, check the scopes, and use it as your password when cloning and pushing.
3. Set your interpreter: *File > Settings > Project > Python Interpreter > Add Interpreter > New VirtualEnv > OK*.
4. Start building.
